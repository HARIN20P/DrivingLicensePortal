Common questions

How long does a new license take?

After your documents and test are cleared, printing and delivery usually take 7 to 14 working days.

Can I change my details after submitting?

Contact your licensing office with your application number. Changes are possible until documents are verified.

What if I fail the driving test?

You can rebook after a waiting period. A re-test fee applies.

When should I renew?

Renew up to a few months before expiry. Late renewal may need a fresh test and extra fees.
